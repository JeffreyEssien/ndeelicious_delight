## What to include

For order questions, include your order number, customer name, scheduled delivery or collection date and a description of the issue. For cancellation requests, contact us in writing by email or through our website contact form.

## Cake cancellation notice

Wedding cakes require at least one calendar month's written notice. Other cakes require at least one week (seven days). Cake payments already made are non-refundable for customer cancellation or change of mind, subject to mandatory consumer rights. Read /refund-policy for the full policy.
