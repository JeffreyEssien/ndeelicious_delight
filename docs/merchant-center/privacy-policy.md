# Ndeeelicious Delight — Privacy policy

Updated: 5 October 2026

## Information we collect

Ndeeelicious Delight collects the information you provide to place an order, request a cake quote, contact us, subscribe to our newsletter or submit a review. This may include your name, email, phone number, delivery address, order details, cake preferences, messages and reference photographs.

## How we use information

We use this information to prepare and fulfil orders, issue quotes and financial documents, communicate payment and order updates, answer enquiries, maintain business records and protect the service against fraud or abuse. Newsletter signup is optional. You may contact us to ask to stop receiving marketing messages.

## Payments and service providers

Stripe processes payment card information through its secure payment service. We store order and payment references and statuses, rather than full card credentials. Our database, storage, hosting and email providers process information needed to operate the website and deliver your order communications. Provider processing may occur outside your province or country.

## Browser storage and analytics

The website stores your basket locally in your browser. Anonymous storefront analytics use a random browser identifier to record selected actions such as product views and basket activity; these analytics payloads do not include names, email addresses, delivery addresses or payment details. This tracking respects the browser's Global Privacy Control signal. Administrative login uses a session cookie. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings, which may remove your saved basket.

## Security and retention

We use access controls and other technical measures to protect information. We keep information for order fulfilment, support, security and applicable record-keeping needs. Order, invoice and receipt records may need to be retained even after a request to delete other information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.
